Community Safety & Trust Guidelines

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

1. Strictly Adults Only (18+)

Ping is designed exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. Minors are strictly prohibited.

2. Text-Only Platform Scope & Exploitation Prevention

Ping is strictly a text-only platform. No native image uploads or video streams exist. Text messages are screened in real time by in-process local neural models (`Xenova/toxic-bert`) and deterministic pattern filters with zero tolerance for exploitation or grooming.

3. Dual-Pass Moderation & 3-Strike Rule

All transmitted messages pass through dual-pass moderation. Offensive language triggers automated warnings on Strikes 1 and 2, followed by an immediate 15-minute device/IP suspension on Strike 3.

4. Protection Tips

  • Never share real names, addresses, or phone numbers with strangers.
  • Never share private financial or social media credentials.
  • Use the in-app Report button immediately if a partner acts inappropriately.

5. Contact

Safety & Abuse Concerns: safety@ping.app
