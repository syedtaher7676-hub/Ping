How to install on iPhone & iPad (Home Screen Web App):

Notice: You are currently in an In-App Browser (e.g. Instagram/TikTok). Tap ••• or ⋮ at the top/bottom and choose Open in Safari first to install.
  1. In Safari, tap the Share button in the bottom toolbar.
  2. Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen ➕.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner to save Ping as an app on your Home Screen.

How to install on Desktop (Chrome / Edge):

Click the Install icon in your browser address bar at the top right, or choose Install Ping in the browser menu.
