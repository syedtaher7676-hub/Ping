Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

1. Zero Chat Log Storage

Conversations on Ping occur in real time and exist strictly in temporary server RAM during active sessions. Raw chat text is never recorded, stored, or archived to disk or permanent databases.

2. Explicit "No Third-Party AI Data Transfer" Clause

AI-powered content moderation runs 100% locally in-process via the embedded ONNX Runtime (`Xenova/toxic-bert`). Your raw chat text is NEVER sent to, processed by, or shared with third-party external AI APIs (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google AI) for processing or model training.

3. Legal Basis for Data Processing (GDPR Article 6(1)(f) Legitimate Interest)

Under Article 6(1)(f) of the EU/UK GDPR (Legitimate Interest), Ping processes anonymized device hashes (`dev_xxxx`) and temporary IP telemetry strictly for platform safety, abuse prevention, 15-minute ban enforcement, and protection against automated botnet swarms.

Data Category Purpose Retention
Raw Chat Text Ephemeral chat stream 0 Seconds (RAM only)
Anonymized Device Hash Ban enforcement & safety Active ban duration
IP Telemetry VPN & proxy protection In-memory 24-hour cache

5. Strict 18+ Adult Policy

Ping is strictly an adults-only service for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from or offer services to minors.
